MiniFreak Technical Controls & Signal-Flow Exploration Roadmap

How levels, curves, slopes, voicing & sidechaining shape the sound + “Try This to Hear It” lab patches

Focus Stage:
_TECHNICAL ROADMAP

End-to-End Signal Architecture, Control Impact & Exploration Lab

Tip: Long-press Preset/Edit (Init Preset) before running any “Try This to Hear It” experiment!
1. Osc Levels, Mix & Wavefolding Physics Gain & Harmonics
  • • Dual-Role Volume Knobs: In standard modes, Osc 1/2 Volume (Shift+Vol = Mix) sets the balance feeding the Analog Filter. High combined volume gently saturates the analog VCF input.
  • • Osc 2 Processor Gain Staging (Pale Blue LED): When Osc 2 is set to a Filter or Destroy, Osc 1 Volume becomes Input Drive into Osc 2, while Osc 2 Volume becomes Dry/Wet Mix!
  • • Why Wavefolding Sounds Subtle on Some Waves: A wavefolder (Waveshaper, BASS, Destroy) folds the peaks of a wave back down across itself. If your source wave is already a bright square/saw or played in a high octave, the folded peaks blend into existing harmonics. Feeding it a low-octave Sine/Triangle reveals dramatic, vocal-like harmonic multiplication.
⚛ Try This to Hear It: Load Init Preset → Set Osc 1 to Waveshaper (Wave=25, Asym=0). Play a low C1/C2 and sweep Timbre (Amount) 0→100 to hear pure wavefolding. Now set Osc 2 to Destroy, set Osc 2 Vol=100%, and sweep Osc 1 Vol to hear input drive!
2. Filter Slopes (12 vs 36 dB) & Key Tracking Frequency & Q
  • • Slope Steepness: The Analog SEM Filter has a gentle 12 dB/octave slope—one octave above Cutoff is only 12 dB quieter, letting airy upper harmonics breathe. Need a surgical wall? Route Osc 1 into Osc 2's Multi Filter set to LP24 or LP36 (36 dB/oct)!
  • • Resonance (Q) & Self-Oscillation: Raising Resonance narrows and boosts the peak right at Cutoff. At 100, the Analog Filter self-oscillates as a pure sine wave even with Osc 1 & 2 Volumes at 0!
  • • Keyboard Tracking (Kbd → Cutoff): Without key tracking, high notes sound muffled and low notes sound overly bright. Setting Matrix Keyboard → Cutoff = +50 locks the filter cutoff 1:1 to the played pitch across all 37 keys.
⚛ Try This to Hear It: Turn Osc 1 & 2 Volume = 0. Turn Analog Filter Resonance = 100. In Matrix, set Keyboard → Cutoff = +50.0. Play the keyboard and tune Cutoff—you are now playing the Analog Filter itself as a sine synthesizer!
3. Voicing Modes, Paraphony & Voice Stealing 6 vs 12 Voices
  • • True Poly (6v) vs. Paraphonic (12v): In Poly, each of the 6 voices has 2 Oscillators + its own Analog VCF + VCA. Switching to Para disables Osc 2 and repurposes all 12 oscillators as 12 voices grouped into 6 Voice Pairs. Each voice has its own digital ADSR, while each pair shares an analog AHR Master Envelope!
  • • Poly Allocation (Sound Edit > Voice): Cycle rotates through all 6 analog filters (subtle vintage variation); Reassign re-uses the exact same voice when you repeat a note (like a real piano string); Reset always grabs Voice 1 first.
  • • Bass-Safe Voice Stealing: Whether Poly Steal is Oldest, Lowest Velo, or None, MiniFreak never steals your lowest held bass note!
⚛ Try This to Hear It: Set a long Release and compare repeatedly tapping one key in Poly Allocation = Cycle (tails stack up across 6 voices and steal) vs. Reassign (each tap cleanly re-strikes the same single voice!).
4. Envelope Curves, Stage Order & Retriggering Time & Transients
  • • VCA Envelope Curve Shapes (Sound Edit > Envelope): Switching Attack to Quick and Decay/Release to Percussive bends the slope so the initial drop is much steeper—turning soft notes into snappy plucks without shortening the tail.
  • • Cycling Env Curvature (Shift + Rise/Fall): 50 = Linear. <50 = Exponential (slow build, sudden snap at end). >50 = Logarithmic (instant punchy rise, gradual settle).
  • • Stage Order (RHF vs. RFH / HRF): In RHF, Hold pauses at 100% peak (trapezoid drone). In RFH or HRF, Hold pauses at 0% floor—creating repeating percussive spikes separated by silence!
  • • Env Reset vs. Env Continue: In Mono/Uni/Para, Reset drops to 0 on every key press (clicky/plucky); Continue rises smoothly from the previous note's current level.
⚛ Try This to Hear It: Route CycEnv → Cutoff (+60) in Loop mode with short Rise/Fall and long Hold. Toggle Sound Edit > CycEnv > Stage Order from RHF (held open filter) to RFH (rhythmic gated plucks!).
5. Expressive Dynamics: Velocity, AT & Kbd Src Touch & Feel
  • • 4 Separate Velocity Paths (Sound Edit > Envelope): By default an Init patch ignores velocity! Dial in Velo > VCA (volume dynamics), Velo > VCF (or Shift + Filter Env knob for brightness), Velo > Env (Matrix depth), and Velo > Time (hard strikes ring out longer!).
  • • Matrix Src VeloAT = Both (Sound Edit > Keyboard): The secret expressive weapon! Velocity sets the initial modulation value on key strike, and pressing harder into Aftertouch smoothly sweeps from that hit value up to 100%.
  • • Kbd Src Modes: Changes what the Matrix Keyboard row sends: Linear (centered at C3), S-Curve (gentle at extremes), Random (new random value per key press), or Voices (fixed offset per voice 1–6 for organic per-voice variation!).
⚛ Try This to Hear It: Set Sound Edit > Keyboard > Kbd Src = Voices, then route Keyboard → Timbre 1 (+25) and Pitch 1+2 (+0.08). Every voice in a chord now has its own vintage analog drift!
6. Matrix Sidechaining & Insert vs. Send FX Mod Depth & Routing
  • • Modulating a Modulation (Sidechaining): Want an LFO or Cycling Env to only kick in when you push the Wheel or press Aftertouch? Hold Assign 1 + turn the Matrix encoder to target an existing Matrix point (e.g. LFO1 → Cutoff), or turn Preset to select LFO1 AM. Now route Wheel → Assign 1!
  • • Macros as Safe Experimentation Sandboxes: Since Macros (M1 / M2) store 0–100% offsets over up to 4 parameters (including Matrix amounts!), assign risky tweaks to a Macro—returning the strip to 0 always restores your saved patch intact.
  • • Insert vs. Send FX Topologies (Sound Edit + FX Select): Insert runs FX1 → FX2 → FX3 in series (e.g., Reverb into Distortion/Flanger for shoegaze walls). Send taps the signal in parallel so Delay and Reverb don't smear each other or get crushed by FX 3!
⚛ Try This to Hear It: Set FX 1 = Reverb (Hall) and FX 2 = Distortion (Dual Fold). Play a chord with Reverb set to Insert (huge distorted reverb tail), then switch Reverb Routing to Send (gritty dry synth sitting in a crystal-clean hall!).
