_OSCILLATORS

Sound Palette: What Is the Vibe & When to Explore Each Engine?

OLED Icons: 1. Wave = Test Tube • 2. Timbre = Bottle • 3. Shape = Flask Freak Out: You can modulate Oscillator Type in the Matrix!
14 Shared Oscillator Engines (Available on Both Osc 1 & Osc 2) Knobs: 1=Wave | 2=Timbre | 3=Shape
Engine The Vibe / When to Use 1: Wave • 2: Timbre • 3: Shape Field Notes & Sweet Spots
BasicWaves Classic 60s/70s analog breads-and-butter, hollow PWM leads & deep sub-anchored basses. 1: Morph (0=Sqr, 50=Saw, 100=2×Saw +1oct) • 2: Sym (PW <50, Phase >50) • 3: Sub (-1oct Sine) Max PW square = clean retro tone; narrow PW = buzzy/hollow. 2×Saw needs phase (Sym > 50) to pop. The -1oct sine sub adds huge warmth.
SuperWave Lush, wide JP-8000 trance stacks, warm detuned pads, or soft swirling clouds. 1: Wave (Saw/Sqr/Tri/Sin) • 2: Detune (spread) • 3: Volume (copies vs base) Triangle acts like a soft sine wave. High Detune veers into “angry bees”—pull back 3: Volume to keep copies in check!
VAnalog Dual-wave virtual analog (Pulse + Saw) capable of tearing hard-sync leads & wide intervals. 1: Detune (Saw vs Pulse ±2oct) • 2: Shape (Narrow→Sqr→Sync) • 3: Wave (Tri→Saw) Broader tonal range than BasicWaves—great for harmonic exploration and aggressive sync-like sweeps on 2: Shape.
Harmo Shimmering additive synthesis, drawbar organs, and evolving glass/prism textures. 1: Content (8-harmonic tables) • 2: Sculpting (Sin→Tri harmonics) • 3: Chorus Sculpting adds peaks and shifts offset; high Content dominates fundamental. Built-in Chorus adds movement (stacking Chorus FX = a lot!).
Harm (NE) Additive harmonics pushed through an analog-style rectifier & phase-noise circuit. 1: Spread (50=Uni, 0/100=±1oct) • 2: Rectify (fold) • 3: Noise (phase clip) Ideal when you want to blend controlled harmonic tone adjustments with wavefold-style edge; values between 0/50/100 act like a balanced modulator.
Waveshaper West-Coast Buchla wavefolding & cycle-asymmetry for rubbery, nasal, overtone-rich leads. 1: Wave (shaper wave) • 2: Amount (fold depth) • 3: Asym (Sin→Saw tilt) Wavefolding is hardest to hear at extreme ends of Wave—play low notes with mid Wave settings, or modulate all 3 knobs with LFOs!
BASS (NE) Massive quadrature sine+cosine bass engine with overdrive and 2-stage asymmetric wavefolder. 1: Saturate (cosine drive) • 2: Fold (2-stage fold) • 3: Noise (inter-stage PM) Awesome for “harmony explosions” from a sub foundation. Built-in inter-stage noise behaves quirkily and adds organic grit.
SawX (NE) Super-charged sawtooth phase-modulated by decimated white noise + thick chorus. 1: SawMod (mod intensity) • 2: Shape (chorus depth) • 3: Noise (radio grit) Sounds like blowing through a progressively smaller/larger tube. Pro Tip: Modulate 2: Shape with a slow LFO for a unique sweeping width.
Two Op. FM DX7-style glassy electric pianos, woody mallets, metallic bells, and growling FM basses. 1: Ratio (2:1 oct, 1.5:1 5th, or inharm) • 2: Amount (overtones) • 3: Feedback Very sensitive to precise ratios. Pro Tip: Set both Osc 1 & Osc 2 to Two Op. FM to build a 4-Operator FM synth with two feedback loops!
KarplusStr Physical model of plucked harp/guitar strings, bowed cello tones, and struck resonances. 1: Bow (pluck vs continuous bow) • 2: Position (center vs edge) • 3: Decay Fast Attack = harp/string pluck; raise ADSR Attack time + turn up Bow to hear a realistic drawn bow!
Modal Exciter + damped resonator physical model for marimbas, xylophones, kalimbas & church bells. 1: Inharm (woody→metallic) • 2: Timbre (exciter bright) • 3: Decay (damping) Low Decay = tight xylophone/palm-mute plucks; high Decay + high Inharm = shimmering metallic bells.
Formant Granular particles routed through dual fixed-frequency vocal filters for choir & talking leads. 1: Interval (formant ratio) • 2: Formant (base freq) • 3: Shape (width) Static settings can feel subtle—comes alive when you sweep Interval and Formant with an Envelope, LFO, or Wheel!
Speech 1970s Speak & Spell / Kraftwerk robot voice singing vowels, colors, numbers & NATO words. 1: Type (word bank) • 2: Timbre (formant shift) • 3: Word (select word) Route an LFO set to SnH (Sample & Hold) to 3: Shape (Word) so every key press speaks a random word at the played pitch!
Noise Percussive transients, wind/ocean sweeps, lo-fi crackle, or pitched noise-plus-wave layers. 1: Type (4 zones in 25% steps: Particle→White→Metallic) • 2: Rate • 3: Balance Balance blends pure noise → +Sine → +Triangle → +Square so one Osc provides both air/grit and fundamental pitch at once.
Osc 1 Exclusive Engines (Samples, Wavetables & 7 Granulars) Shift+Type = Select Source
• Wavetable: Scans 32 cycle tables with built-in filter. 1: Wave (scan cycle) • 2: Timbre (symmetrical PW) • 3: Shape (1-pole LPF/HPF).
• Sample: Plays factory acoustic/synth samples forward or backward! 1: Start • 2: Length/Dir (50–100 = Forward, 49–0 = Reverse) • 3: Loop. Note: Set Start = 100 to hear the full sample in reverse!
• Audio In: Routes rear jack (Mic/Synth, or MiniFreak's own output for self-feedback!) into synth voice. 1: Fold (input level @ Decimate=0) • 2: Decimate (key-tracked) • 3: Noise. Must hold a key / Latch Arp to open VCA!
7 Granular Engines (“Like a Built-In Little Arp for Any Sample!”) Knob 1 = Always Start Pos
1. Cloud: Textural ambient grain clouds (2:Density, 3:Chaos)
2. Hit: Sharp rhythmic stutter blasts (2:Density, 3:Shape/Hold)
3. Frozen: Ethereal icy time-freeze (2:Size, 3:Chaos)
4. Skan: Time-stretch playhead sweep (2:Scan Spd, 3:Chaos)
5. Particles: Hypnotic psychedelic layers (2:Density, 3:Chaos)
6. Lick: 1/16 tempo-synced groove (2:Size, 3:Chaos)
7. Raster: Tempo-synced beat-repeat chopper (2:Rate 1/2–1/32, 3:Chaos)
Osc 2 Exclusive Engines & Audio Processors Pale Blue LED = Osc1 → Osc2
• Chords: 1-note chord machine with inversions! 1: Interval (Oct, 5th, sus4, m, m7, m9, m11, 69, M9, M7, M) • 2: Inv/Trsp (voice inversions) • 3: Waveform (waves 0–50, wavetable 50–100).
• FM/RM: Osc 1 frequency- & ring-modulates Osc 2 for clangorous metallic sum/difference tones. 1: Wave (Osc 2 wave) • 2: FreqMod • 3: RingMod.
• Multi Filter: Digital filter with gentle-to-steep slopes (6, 12, 24, 36 dB/oct). 1: Cutoff • 2: Reso • 3: Mode (LP, BP, HP, Notch). Map Kbd → Osc2 Wave @ 50 to key-track!
• Surgeon Filter: Scalpel parametric EQ to carve wolf tones or boost a resonant peak. 1: Cutoff • 2: Spread (width in BP/Notch) • 3: Mode.
• Comb Filter: Short delay feedback creating hollow, flanger/plucked resonances (auto key-tracked!). 1: Cutoff • 2: Gain • 3: Damping.
• Phaser Filter: Manual all-pass phase notch bank (sweeps only when you modulate Cutoff!). 1: Cutoff • 2: Feedback • 3: Poles (2–12 stages = 1–6 notches).
• Destroy: Brutal 3-stage mangler on Osc 1. 1: Fold • 2: Decimate (auto key-tracked crackle) • 3: Bitcrush.
_DIGITAL FX

3-Slot Digital Effects Guide (FX 1 → FX 2 → FX 3): Vibe, Subtypes & Sound Recipes

Shift + Turn Type/Sub = Load Subtype (reconfigures hidden internal parameters!) Note: Only 1 Delay, 1 Reverb & 1 Multi Comp allowed per patch
FX Type The Vibe / When to Use Knobs: 1:Time • 2:Intensity • 3:Amount Subtypes (Shift+Type) & Pro Tips
1. Chorus Widens & thickens stereo image; turns thin mono waves into ensemble pads. 1: Rate • 2: Depth (wet internal mix) • 3: Dry/Wet Default, Lush, Dark, Shaded, Single. Tip: Depth ≠ Dry/Wet! Set Dry/Wet = 100% + fast Rate for true pitch vibrato.
2. Super Unison Stacks up to 6 LFO-detuned copies over dry signal without using polyphony voices! 1: Detune (amt + LFO speed) • 2: LPF/HPF • 3: Dry/Wet Classic, Ravey, Soli, Slow, Slow Trig, Wide Trig, Mono Trig, Wavy. Trig subtypes reset phase on legato notes!
3. Phaser 70s swirling all-pass phase sweeps, cosmic pads, or rhythmic sample-and-hold steps. 1: Rate • 2: Feedback (resonance) • 3: Dry/Wet Default, Space, SnH (+ Sync versions of each). Use SnH Sync for tempo-locked stepped filter motion!
4. Flanger Jet-plane metallic comb sweeps and resonant stereo movement. 1: Rate • 2: Feedback • 3: Dry/Wet Default, Default Sync, Silly, Silly Sync. High Feedback screams like a resonant filter.
5. Reverb Places dry synth in a tight room, dark chamber, or endless ambient cathedral. 1: Decay (size) • 2: Damping (HF absorb) • 3: Dry/Wet | Send Default, Long, Hall, Echoes, Room, Dark Room. Switch to Send in Sound Edit > FX to keep subsequent FX dry!
6. Delay Rhythmic dotted-8th echoes, slapback doubling, or stereo ping-pong repeats. 1: Time • 2: Feedback (0=1 slap, 100=inf) • 3: Dry/Wet | Send Digital, Stereo, Ping-Pong, Mono, Filtered, Filtered Ping-Pong (+ Sync versions).
7. Distortion Everything from warm tape saturation and germanium fuzz to dual-wavefolding chaos. 1: Gain (auto-gain leveled!) • 2: HPF/LPF (<0 LP, >0 HP) • 3: Dry/Wet Classic, Soft Clip, Germanium, Dual Fold, Climb, Tape. Auto-gain prevents volume jumps as you crank Gain.
8. Bit Crusher Early-sampler 8-bit crunch and sample-rate aliasing ring/garbage. 1: Decimate (sample rate) • 2: BitDepth • 3: Dry/Wet No subtypes. Blend low Dry/Wet (15–25%) to add crispy top-end fizz to warm pads or basses.
9. 3 Bands EQ Fast mixer-style tone shaping (-15 dB to +6 dB per band). 1: Low Gain • 2: Mid Gain • 3: High Gain Default, Wide, Mid 1K. Great at FX 3 end-of-chain to sculpt final mix weight.
10. Peak EQ Single parametric band to notch out a harsh resonant spike or boost a sweet spot. 1: Freq (30Hz–15kHz) • 2: Gain • 3: Width (Q) No subtypes. Sweep Freq with narrow Width via LFO/Env for a post-VCF formant filter!
11. Multi Comp Multiband compressor (up to 30 internal params) for OTT-style smack &glue. 1: Time (Atk/Rel) • 2: Tone (band balance) • 3: Amount OPP (OTT-style), Bass Ctrl, High Ctrl, Tighter. OPP brings out tiny granular/modal details!
12. Vocoder (v4.0) Ext In: Voice mic modulates synth. Self: Synth vocodes itself like a resonant filter bank! Ext: 1:Time, 2:Intensity/Gate, 3:Amt
Self: 1:Spectrum, 2:Formant/Gate, 3:Amt		 Clean, Vintage (70s/80s), Narrow (resonant), Gated (plucky droplets). Vocoder Self + Gated turns pads into water droplets!
6 “First Point of Entry” Sound-Building Recipes
1. Huge 80s CS-80 / Blade Runner Brass-Pad
Osc 1: VAnalog or BasicWaves (Saw + Sub) • Osc 2: SuperWave (+5 cents fine tune) • Voicing: Poly • Filter: LPF w/ slow Attack Env • FX: Chorus (Lush) → Reverb (Hall). Set Matrix Src VeloAT = Both → Cutoff!
2. West-Coast Buchla Bongos / Pluck Bass
Osc 1: Waveshaper or BASS (low octave) • Filter: LPF wide open • CycEnv: Env mode with snappy logarithmic Fall routed to Osc 1 Timbre (Fold Amount) instead of Filter Cutoff!
3. Organic Bowed Cello → Plucked Harp Morph
Osc 1: KarplusStr • Route Macro 1 to simultaneously raise Osc 1 Wave (Bow) AND lengthen Envelope Attack. At M1=0 it's a plucked harp; sweep M1 up to draw the bow!
4. 4-Operator DX7 Bell / Glass Keys
Osc 1: Two Op. FM (Ratio = 2:1) • Osc 2: Two Op. FM (Ratio = 3.5:1, +12 st) • Env: Fast Attack, long Percussive Decay → Matrix Timbre 1+2 • FX: Super Unison → Delay (Ping-Pong).
5. Instant Granular Breakbeat / Glitch Loop
Osc 1: Lick or Raster (pick a drum/percussion sample via Shift+Type) • Osc 2: Destroy or Comb Filter • FX: Multi Comp (OPP) → Vocoder Self (Gated).
6. 12-Voice Paraphonic String Machine
Osc 1: SawX (modulate Shape with slow LFO 1) • Voicing: Para (unlocks 12 voices!) • FX: Phaser (Space) → Super Unison (Soli) → Reverb.
